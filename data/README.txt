The three SARS-CoV-2 alignments used in the SwiftSNP preprint, as downloaded on 11 October 2026 from the Nextstrain
open builds (https://data.nextstrain.org/files/ncov/open/<region>/aligned.fasta.xz; built by Nextstrain from public
NCBI GenBank and Robert Koch Institute data and released as open data). Nextstrain regenerates these builds, so
fetch_real_data.sh downloads a different set today; these files are the exact sets.

  sarscov2_europe_n2012.fa.xz   europe build, 2,012 genomes
  sarscov2_global_n4637.fa.xz   global build, 4,637 genomes
  sarscov2_n8596.fa.xz          union of the seven regional builds, 8,596 genomes

The V. cholerae and M. tuberculosis alignments are versioned Zenodo records; fetch_real_data.sh downloads them.
